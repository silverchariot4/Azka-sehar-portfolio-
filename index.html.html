ABOUT ME

I'm Azka Sehar — a video editor and motion artist specialising in anime/manga aesthetics, After Effects animation, and high-impact short-form content.

I animate 2D manga panels in After Effects — bringing still frames to life with parallax depth, kinetic typography, and cinematic sound design. It's a niche craft, and I've made it my signature.

Currently managing content for 5 active YouTube channels simultaneously, and have delivered professional work for an international client based in Germany. I move fast, I communicate clearly, and the quality speaks for itself.
